Privacy policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Permit Drive is a personal app for logging supervised practice drives.

  • On-device data. Drives (times, route, distance, supervisor, car, notes) are stored on the user's iPhone.
  • Location. Location is used only while a drive is being recorded, to log the route and distance.
  • Google account. With the user's permission, the app uses the Google Sheets API solely to read and write the one practice-log spreadsheet configured in the app. It does not access other files or Google data.
  • No sharing. No data is sent to the developer or any third party. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Storage and revocation. The Google sign-in token is stored in the iOS Keychain. Access can be removed at any time in the app's Settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: open an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
